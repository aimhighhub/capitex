How Capitex works

Every step of your money's journey is visible, trackable, and explained. No black boxes. No surprises. Just consistent growth.

1

Create your account & verify identity (KYC)

Sign up with your email in under 2 minutes. We then walk you through a 4-step KYC process to verify who you are and unlock your full limits. KYC is required by financial regulations to protect you and the platform.

✉️ Email Verified 🪪 ID Upload 🤳 Liveness Check 🏠 Address Proof
KYC takes 5–10 minutes. Document review takes 24–48 hours. You can start with Tier 0 access ($500/month limit) while we review.

2

Set your auto-save schedule

Choose how often you want to invest: Daily (from $25), Weekly (from $50), or Monthly (from $155). You decide the day, the amount, and the asset mix. Then set it and forget it — Capitex does the rest automatically.

💡 Smart scheduling tip
You'll receive a 24-hour advance email before every scheduled deposit. If a payment fails, we retry 3 times before pausing your schedule — a clear fix prompt appears in your dashboard.
3

Allocate across 10 asset classes

Split your deposit across Stocks, ETFs, Gold, Real Estate REITs, Treasury Bonds, Crypto, USDC Yield, Agriculture, Commodities, and Gift Card investments. Percentages must total 100%. You can change your allocation anytime — changes apply on the next cycle.

📈 US Stocks · 8–14%/yr
🥇 Digital Gold · 5–10%/yr
📊 ETFs · 7–12%/yr
💵 USDC Yield · 4–8% APY
4

Payment is processed & verified instantly

Your payment method is matched to your country automatically. Every transaction goes through a cryptographic webhook signature verification — we never credit your account until the gateway confirms the payment. You'll get an email receipt immediately.

🌐 Stripe · US, EU, UK, AU 🦋 Flutterwave · Africa 💵 USDC · Global 💳 Paystack · Ghana, Kenya 🎁 Gift Cards · Worldwide
5

Watch your portfolio grow — in real time

Your dashboard updates instantly after every deposit. Track daily performance with charts, see your allocation split, monitor each asset's return, and review your full transaction history. Everything is in one place.

📊 What your dashboard shows
Total portfolio value · 30-day performance chart · Asset allocation donut · All transactions · Active schedules · Withdrawal history
6

Withdraw anytime — clear timeline, no surprises

Submit a withdrawal request from your dashboard anytime. Choose your destination: bank account, crypto wallet, or mobile money. Admin reviews within 1–3 business days. You'll receive email updates at every status change and can track it live.
